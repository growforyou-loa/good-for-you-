Who’s behind Good for You

Good for You started with a simple idea: everyone has feelings they struggle to explain, and the right words can make them easier to carry. We write poetry in Hindi and Marathi because those languages feel closest to the heart, and we write our books in English so more people can read them. Everything here is made by hand, with care, and shared for free.
