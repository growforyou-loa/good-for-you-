Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

At Good for You, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle your information as a creator or a user of our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you use our platform:

  • Account Data: Name and email via Firebase Authentication (Google) for library access.
  • Creator Data: Content, pen names, and payment contact details for those using the "Publish Your Content" feature.
  • Service Data: Topics and personal preferences shared via our customization forms for poems and subliminals.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to provide our services, specifically:

  • To credit authors for their work in our E-Library.
  • To process and record earnings for creators (80% revenue share model).
  • To personalize custom subliminals and speeches based on your specific requests.
  • To manage the "Live Community" discussion via Disqus.

3. Content Ownership & Creators

When you submit a poem or story to be published, you retain the copyright. We only collect the necessary information to display your work and facilitate the payout of your earnings every Sunday.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to enhance your experience:

  • Netlify & Google Firebase: For secure hosting and user authentication.
  • Disqus: To power our community discussions (Disqus may use cookies for login purposes).

5. Data Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your private consultation details and customized topics are kept confidential and used only for creative fulfillment.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about your data or wish to request its removal, please contact us at the Help Desk.
